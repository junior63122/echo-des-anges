Three SeaBubbles alumni launch NepTech to design low-carbon boats

Set up in Aix-en-Provence by Corentin Bigot, Tanguy Goetz and Clément Rousset, the start-up is among the 2020 award winners of Réseau Entreprendre Provence.

By Nathan Belkacem2020Sources : CFNews, Réseau Entreprendre ProvenceLire en français

They cut their teeth at SeaBubbles, the company known for its electric “flying boats”. Corentin Bigot, Tanguy Goetz and Clément Rousset founded NepTech in Aix-en-Provence this year to market their own boat concept, specialist website CFNews reported.

The young company aims to design low-carbon vessels, including hydrogen-powered ones.

NepTech is among the 2020 award winners of Réseau Entreprendre Provence, which supports business founders through interest-free personal loans and mentoring by experienced business leaders.

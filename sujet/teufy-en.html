Teufy

Going-out app for the French Riviera and the Var

Launched in 2021 by Junior William Joyant, Teufy is a going-out app listing nearby parties, festivals and deals while giving venues more visibility. It has brought together more than 200 partners across the French Riviera and the Var, and is due to be completely overhauled by 2027-2028.
